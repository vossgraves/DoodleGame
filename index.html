HOW TO PLAY

🖱 DESKTOP

  • WASD move · Mouse aim
  • LMB fire / slash · Shift sprint
  • C / Ctrl / Space dash (i-frames!) · R reload
  • F quick katana slash · fill the gauge → dash-slash
  • G grenade — hold for a longer throw
  • 1–5 / wheel weapons · P / Esc pause · M music

📱 MOBILE

  • Left stick move (push far = sprint)
  • Right stick aim & fire
  • FIRE shoots nearest enemy (aim assist)
  • DASH dodge · 💣 hold to lob further
  • RLD reload · ▤ next weapon
ZOMBIES: survive the waves · coins drop from kills · between waves pick 1 upgrade or buy heals.
BOSS every 5th wave. Stay moving. The horde hears you.
